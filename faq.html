01 / GENERAL
What is AETHER TECH?

AETHER TECH is a technology-focused digital solutions platform providing professional websites, digital systems, software solutions, automation and technology infrastructure for businesses and organizations.

What type of clients can work with AETHER TECH?

We can work with individuals, startups, small businesses, educational organizations and growing enterprises depending on the project requirements.

Do you only create websites?

No. Websites are one part of our services. Depending on the project, solutions can also include digital systems, automation, integrations, dashboards and custom technology solutions.

02 / SERVICES
What services do you provide?

Services can include professional website development, responsive UI/UX, digital systems, automation, backend development, integrations, infrastructure planning and custom solutions.

Can you build a completely custom website?

Yes. Custom projects can be planned according to the required design, pages, functionality, integrations and business requirements.

Will the website work on mobile phones?

Responsive design can be implemented so that the website adapts to desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes.

Can you add new features later?

Yes. A properly structured project can be expanded later with additional pages, features, integrations or other functionality.

03 / TECHNOLOGY
What technologies do you use?

Technology selection depends on the project. Frontend, backend, database, hosting and integration technologies are selected according to functionality, scalability and maintenance requirements.

Can you integrate third-party services?

Third-party integrations can be implemented when the required service provides suitable APIs or integration options.

Can you create admin dashboards?

Yes. Custom administrative interfaces and dashboards can be developed when required by the project.

04 / PRICING
How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, design complexity, functionality, integrations, backend requirements and overall project scope. Visit the Pricing page for starting packages.

Is the price shown on the Pricing page final?

Pricing shown on the website should be treated as a starting estimate. A final quote is based on the actual requirements of the project.

Can I request a custom quote?

Yes. If your requirements don't fit one of the standard packages, you can request a custom quote through the Contact page.

05 / PROCESS
What happens after I contact you?

We first understand your requirements, objectives and expected functionality. Based on the scope, the project can then be planned and discussed in detail.

Do you discuss the project before development?

Yes. Understanding the project before development helps establish the required features, structure, technology and scope.

Do you test the project before launch?

Project testing can include checking responsiveness, functionality, navigation, forms and other important components before deployment.

06 / SUPPORT
Do you provide maintenance and support?

Maintenance and ongoing support can be discussed according to the project's requirements and selected service arrangement.

Can you help after the website goes live?

Yes. Post-launch assistance and future improvements can be discussed depending on the project and support requirements.
